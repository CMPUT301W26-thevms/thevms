# TheVMS App Demo Script (Fast-Paced)

**Project Name:** TheVMS (Visitor Management System)
**Group Size:** 6 Members
**Total Duration:** 5 Minutes (4m Presentation + 1m Q&A)

---

## Part 1: Intro & The Vision (40s)

**Speaker 1:**

* **Action:** Show Splash/Login screen.
* **Script:** "Hi everyone. We are presenting **TheVMS**, a comprehensive Visitor Management System.
  Our goal was to eliminate the chaos of event coordination—from messy attendee lists to lost
  invites. Built with Android and Firebase, TheVMS is a secure, real-time solution for seamless
  gathering. Let's dive in."

---

## Part 2: The Entrant Journey (40s)

**Speaker 2:**

* **Action:** Show Profile & Search Fragment.
* **Script:** "For Entrants, it's about speed. After a quick profile setup, the **Search Fragment**
  uses real-time Firestore queries to find events instantly. Whether it's a small workshop or a
  large conference, finding your next event is just a tap away."

---

## Part 3: Connectivity (40s)

**Speaker 3:**

* **Action:** Show Notifications & Maps integration.
* **Script:** "Engagement is key. Our **Inbox system** keeps users updated instantly. We’ve also
  integrated **Google Maps** directly—providing one-tap navigation to event locations. We ensure
  attendees get the right info at the right time."

---

## Part 4: Organizing Events (40s)

**Speaker 4:**

* **Action:** Show 'Create Event' & 'My Events' list.
* **Script:** "Organizers get powerful, simple tools. Creating an event with custom capacity and
  schedules takes seconds. The **Organizer Dashboard** then provides a high-level view of all hosted
  events, making management effortless."

---

## Part 5: Smart Logistics (40s)

**Speaker 5:**

* **Action:** Show Attendee List & Replacement logic.
* **Script:** "We handle the hard stuff: waiting lists and cancellations. Our **Replacement system**
  automates logistics if someone drops out. Plus, with integrated **Comments**, organizers can
  engage with their community in real-time."

---

## Part 6: Admin Control & Wrap-up (40s)

**Speaker 6:**

* **Action:** Show Admin Panel (Profiles/Logs).
* **Script:** "Finally, our **Admin Dashboard** ensures platform safety, allowing for profile
  moderation and system auditing. TheVMS is a scalable, end-to-end solution for modern events. Thank
  you! We'll now take your questions for the final minute."
